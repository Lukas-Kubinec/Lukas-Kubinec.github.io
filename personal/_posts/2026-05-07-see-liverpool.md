---
title: See Liverpool
---

Interactive-Educational video game, where the player must explore the level, which is based on the Liverpool's Waterfront and its historic landmarks. 

The goal of the game is to immerse the player in the city’s rich heritage, as well as the influential figures who drove its global fame. 

Using the information learnt, the players must synthesize discovered historical insights, look for hidden collectibles and take in the great variety of architecture, that can only be found in Liverpool.

[Play on Itch.IO](https://lukey-uk.itch.io/see-liverpool)
