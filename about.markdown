I am a Software and Game programmer driven by a fascination with how complex systems create seamless interactive experiences. 
Currently a student at Edge Hill University. 

I balance academic rigor with real-world operational problem-solving. 
My work focuses on building robust, scalable code that bridges the gap between technical architecture and engaging gameplay.
Whether I am optimising a C++ logic or scripting dynamic gameplay systems in C#, I approach every project with a focus on performance and player feel. 

My background at Amazon has instilled a deep appreciation for efficiency and systematic thinking, which I apply directly to my software development process. I thrive in environments that challenge me to solve logic puzzles and create tools that empower other developers.
I am constantly exploring new technologies and refining my craft through personal projects and collaborative ventures. My goal is to contribute to the next generation of games and software that push the boundaries of what is possible. When I am not coding, I am usually deconstructing game mechanics or looking for ways to streamline workflows.
